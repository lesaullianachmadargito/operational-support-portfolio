# Draft office material record work instruction

Independent application exercise by Les' Aullian Achmad Argito. Revision 0.1, 20 October 2026. **Draft — approval pending.** This is not a company SOP, aircraft-maintenance instruction or aviation-compliance document.

## Scope
Record movements of ordinary office consumables in a fictional administrative setting. Excludes aircraft components, controlled stores, maintenance release, procurement approval and inventory valuation.

## Proposed recording steps
1. Confirm the material ID, description, unit and reporting period against the authorised reference list.
2. Retrieve the previous confirmed closing balance for the opening entry. If unavailable, leave the field missing and request reconciliation; do not substitute zero.
3. Record received and issued quantities against dated source references. Retain the original evidence and avoid counting the same movement twice.
4. Calculate closing quantity as opening plus received minus issued. Keep units consistent; this demonstration uses whole reams, rolls, pieces and packs.
5. Investigate negative balances and missing inputs. Do not silently change the opening balance to make the total positive.
6. Compare a complete non-negative balance with the agreed review level. Flag it for review rather than issuing an automatic purchase instruction.
7. Record the responsible person, due date, question and resolution evidence for outstanding items.
8. Submit the reconciled register for review. Retain the version, review date and reviewer in the organisation's approved filing system.

## Proposed document control
Keep draft, in-review, approved and superseded copies distinguishable. Approval needs the authorised owner's decision and a reference; a dropdown selection alone does not approve a document. Follow the organisation's actual retention and access rules. The example does not prescribe legal retention periods.

## Limits
The portfolio uses aggregate movements, not a transaction ledger. It has no authentication, audit log, approval workflow or backend. It is unsuitable for actual operational control. All edits reset when the browser tab reloads.
